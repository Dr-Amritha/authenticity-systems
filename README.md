# Authenticity Lens

**A human-centred, three-layer provenance inventory framework for synthetic media verification and epistemic security.**

## The Problem

Generative AI systems can now reproduce the aesthetic conventions, narrative structures, and emotional credibility signals historically associated with authentic documentary evidence — including eyewitness testimony, conflict footage, journalistic reporting, and archival records. People can no longer rely on how content looks and feels to judge whether it is real.

Existing verification tools respond to this by detecting technical artefacts and producing probability scores — telling users a piece of content is "67% likely synthetic" without explaining why or what to do with that information. These tools are losing the arms race with generative systems, and they displace human judgement rather than supporting it.

When verification is delegated entirely to automated systems, human beings become disconnected from the responsibility to act. This is particularly critical because the primary function of documentary artefacts is not historical archiving but the call to action they provoke in their audience — the moral, civic, and social response that authentic evidence demands. When that connection between evidence and human responsibility is broken, democratic accountability suffers.

---

## The Intervention

The Authenticity Lens is a structured, human-centred verification framework and proof-of-concept platform that guides users through a **three-layer provenance inventory** to assess the authenticity of media content in a transparent, interpretable, and contestable way.

The framework is designed not to replace human judgement but to scaffold it — walking journalists, educators, fact-checkers, and citizens through a structured reasoning process that makes verification participatory, accountable, and legible to non-technical users.

### The Three Layers

| Layer | What it assesses | Who assesses it |
|---|---|---|
| **Technical markers** | Metadata, compression patterns, generative artefacts, watermark signals | Tool-assisted |
| **Aesthetic markers** | Documentary framing conventions, camera behaviour, environmental sound, lighting consistency, visual credibility signals | Human-guided structured assessment |
| **Narrative markers** | Testimonial structure, emotional urgency, eyewitness conventions, symbolic framing, consistency of account | Human-guided structured assessment |

Together these layers produce a **structured provenance inventory** — a reasoned authenticity assessment that identifies which specific markers raised concern, across which layers, and why. This is fundamentally different from a probability score.

---

## Why This Matters

Current verification tools share a foundational limitation: they treat authenticity as a computational classification problem. The Authenticity Lens treats it as a **human capacity problem** — and addresses what existing tools cannot:

- Synthetic media increasingly derives persuasive power not from technical imperfection but from successfully imitating documentary aesthetics and narrative conventions
- Automated detection outputs are uninterpretable, uncontestable, and inaccessible to non-technical users
- No existing tool integrates aesthetic and narrative credibility analysis with technical detection
- Verification tools built for forensic specialists do not serve the journalists, educators, and citizens who most need them

## Development Roadmap

The Authenticity Lens is being developed across three parallel layers:

### Layer 1 — GTC Prototype (2026, current)
A focused, testable proof-of-concept implementation of the three-layer provenance inventory walkthrough, developed through the Global Trust Challenge Prototyping Phase. Demonstrates that the framework produces more interpretable and actionable authenticity assessments than existing detection tools.

### Layer 2 — MSCA Research Platform (planned, 2027–2029)
A fully validated academic platform developed through a Marie Skłodowska-Curie Postdoctoral Fellowship proposal, with **CYENS Centre of Excellence / Cyprus University of Technology confirmed as the host institution**. Built through rigorous participatory design with educators, journalists, and media literacy practitioners. Free to use, peer-reviewed, and cross-culturally evaluated.

### Layer 3 — Smart Story Labs Commercial Product (post-2029)
An enterprise-grade, institutionally licensed platform commercialised through Smart Story Labs, with advanced features, API integrations, and full product development behind it.
